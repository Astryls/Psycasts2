XML Awakening Triggers (for modders)

Trigger Awakening and Enlightenment tiers from your own XML - drugs, abilities, rituals, artifacts, incidents, conditions, thoughts, precepts and quest signals - without writing a line of C#.

Every surface funnels into the same entry point, so the full Psycasts² flow always runs: colonists get the letter and the tiered card-pick window; everyone else (or anything marked silent) gets the quiet grant used by enemy psycasters - tier, psylink levels, auto-picked paths, full psyfocus. A pawn already at or above the target tier is unaffected, so triggers are always safe to re-apply. C# mods can call PsycastSynergies.AwakeningTrigger.Fire(pawn, tier, silent) directly.

The shared vocabulary

All surfaces are configured by one DefModExtension attached to your def:

<modExtensions>
  <li Class="PsycastSynergies.AwakeningTriggerExtension">
    <tier>1</tier>
  </li>
</modExtensions>
FieldDefaultUsed byMeaning

minAge13allMinimum biological age.

1. Trigger hediffs - the universal hook

Anything that can apply a hediff can trigger awakening. Ready-made: PS_AwakeningTrigger (Tier I), PS_AwakeningTriggerTierII, PS_AwakeningTriggerTierIII. The hediff fires once and removes itself, whether or not the chance roll takes. Example - an awakening brew:

<ingestible>
  <outcomeDoers>
    <li Class="IngestionOutcomeDoer_GiveHediff">
      <hediffDef>PS_AwakeningTrigger</hediffDef>
      <severity>1</severity>
    </li>
  </outcomeDoers>
</ingestible>

The same def name works in ability effects (CompAbilityEffect_GiveHediff), surgery outcomes, ritual outcome effecters, genes, or a scenario's forced hediff. For your own odds/tier/letter, define your own HediffDef with <li Class="PsycastSynergies.HediffCompProperties_TriggerAwakening"/> in comps plus the extension.

2. Usable items

An artifact that awakens its user on activation:

<ThingDef ParentName="ResourceBase">
  <defName>MyMod_AwakeningShard</defName>
  <label>awakening shard</label>
  ...
  <comps>
    <li Class="CompProperties_Usable">
      <useJob>UseItem</useJob>
      <useLabel>Commune with the shard</useLabel>
      <useDuration>600</useDuration>
    </li>
    <li Class="CompProperties_UseEffectDestroySelf"/>
    <li Class="PsycastSynergies.CompProperties_UseEffectTriggerAwakening"/>
  </comps>
  <modExtensions>
    <li Class="PsycastSynergies.AwakeningTriggerExtension"><tier>1</tier></li>
  </modExtensions>
</ThingDef>

Ineligible pawns (already at the tier, too young) are refused at the float-menu level.

3. Incidents

PS_Awakening ships with baseChance 0: it never fires naturally, but storyteller mods, scenario parts, quests and debug Do incident can invoke it. It awakens the colonist deepest into awakening meditation (fallback: random eligible). Define your own IncidentDef with <workerClass>PsycastSynergies.IncidentWorker_TriggerAwakening</workerClass> and your own extension to change tier, targeting or the letter - and give it a real baseChance if you want it storyteller-driven.

4. The Awakening Surge condition

PS_AwakeningSurge (class PsycastSynergies.GameCondition_AwakeningSurge) is a map condition: while active, meditation awakening and breakthrough odds are multiplied by breakthroughMult (default ×3), and meditating non-psycasters roll chancePerDay (default 25%/day) to awaken outright. Conditions never start themselves - trigger it via the inert wrapper incident PS_AwakeningSurgeIncident (1.5-3 days), from a quest (QuestNode_GameCondition), or start it in C# with GameConditionMaker. Multiple surge defs stack multiplicatively; feel free to define your own with different numbers.

5. Thought-carried triggers

Attach the extension to any ThoughtDef (memory or situational): every colonist currently holding that thought rolls chancePerDay. Great for "psychic ecstasy" ritual memories or eerie-place moodlets:

<ThoughtDef>
  <defName>MyMod_TouchedTheVoid</defName>
  ...
  <modExtensions>
    <li Class="PsycastSynergies.AwakeningTriggerExtension">
      <chancePerDay>0.10</chancePerDay>
    </li>
  </modExtensions>
</ThoughtDef>

6. Precept-carried triggers

Attach the extension to a PreceptDef: every colonist whose ideoligion contains the precept rolls chancePerDay. Set scaleWithCertainty so devout believers awaken sooner, and onlyWhileMeditating to keep it earned:

<modExtensions>
  <li Class="PsycastSynergies.AwakeningTriggerExtension">
    <chancePerDay>0.08</chancePerDay>
    <onlyWhileMeditating>true</onlyWhileMeditating>
    <scaleWithCertainty>true</scaleWithCertainty>
  </li>
</modExtensions>

7. Quest signals

QuestScriptDef authors can awaken quest pawns the moment a signal fires. Pawns come from slate refs (pawn / pawns) or, if omitted, from the signal's SUBJECT argument:

<li Class="PsycastSynergies.QuestNode_TriggerAwakening">
  <inSignal>pilgrim.LeftMap</inSignal>
  <pawn>$pilgrim</pawn>
  <tier>2</tier>
  <silent>false</silent>   <!-- optional; chance also supported -->
</li>
Tier II/III triggers bypass the pilgrimage chains by design - they're for content that provides its own journey (a grand ritual, a boss reward, a quest chain). Prefer Tier I unless your content has earned the jump.

8. Owning the whole ladder: TieringOverrideDef

Building a complete replacement progression? Ship one def and Psycasts² hands the ladder over to you: the disable flags switch off the built-in earn paths, while the tier system itself - the tier hediff, card picks, spec points, and every trigger surface above - stays fully live as your progression's engine. The reskin fields rename the ladder everywhere it appears (letters, health tab, card picks, spec UI) and re-cost its spec-point rewards.

<PsycastSynergies.TieringOverrideDef>
  <defName>MyMod_Tiering</defName>
  <disableBuiltInTiering>true</disableBuiltInTiering>  <!-- umbrella: all flags below -->
  <tierNames>
    <li>Initiate</li>
    <li>Adept</li>
    <li>Master</li>
  </tierNames>
  <tierNameBeyondFormat>Master {ROMAN}</tierNameBeyondFormat>
  <tierSpecPoints><li>0</li><li>2</li><li>3</li></tierSpecPoints>
</PsycastSynergies.TieringOverrideDef>
FieldEffect
disableBuiltInTieringUmbrella - every disable flag below at once (total handoff).
disableMeditationAwakeningNon-psycasters no longer Awaken (Tier I) from meditation. Psycaster level breakthroughs are untouched.
disableTranscendenceIlluminated pawns no longer climb Tier 4+ from meditation.
disablePilgrimagesThe Tier II/III pilgrimage quest chains are never offered.
disableExternalPsylinkAwakeningEmpire bestowing / anima linking no longer trigger Awakening.
disableEnemyTiersHostile psycasters no longer spawn tiered.
disableRandomAwakenedSpawnsNo random fresh-Awakened pawn generation.
disablePsylinkGateStop stripping generated psylinks from untiered pawns - use this if your mod grants psylinks its own way.
tierNamesDisplay names for tiers 1..N, replacing Awakened / Enlightened / Illuminated.
tierNameBeyondFormatName for tiers past the list; {TIER} = number, {ROMAN} = numeral. Unset keeps "Transcendent {ROMAN}".
tierSpecPointsSpec points granted on reaching tier 1..N (past the list reuses the last entry). Unset keeps built-in rewards.

Disables are honored regardless of player settings - affected settings rows show an amber "overridden by <your mod>" note so players know who owns progression. Multiple override defs merge: any mod's disable wins; the first mod supplying tierNames/tierSpecPoints wins those (a log warning names any loser). Everything is queryable from C# via PsycastSynergies.TieringControl.

9. Shipping your own balance: BalanceOverrideDef

Modpack and addon authors can retune the synergy graph without touching Psycasts²'s files. Ship one def in your own mod's Defs and it folds into the balance overlay for every player of the pack. It layers above the mod's own baked defaults, but under a player's personal in-game Balance-editor edits, so players keep the final say.

<PsycastSynergies.BalanceOverrideDef>
  <defName>MyPack_PsycastBalance</defName>

  <!-- Per-ability primary: which stat the ability's OWN levels scale, plus a strength multiplier. -->
  <primaries>
    <li><ability>VPE_Firestorm</ability><stat>Power</stat><strength>1.5</strength></li>
    <li><ability>VPE_BladeFocus</ability><stat>none</stat></li>      <!-- no primary scaling -->
  </primaries>

  <!-- Per-edge empower: how a SOURCE ability feeds a TARGET ability (stat + strength). -->
  <empowers>
    <li><source>VPE_Flame</source><target>VPE_Firestorm</target><stat>Radius</stat><strength>0.5</strength></li>
  </empowers>

  <!-- Full replacement of a target ability's synergy-source list. -->
  <sources>
    <li><ability>VPE_Firestorm</ability><from><li>VPE_Flame</li><li>VPE_Ignite</li></from></li>
  </sources>
</PsycastSynergies.BalanceOverrideDef>
FieldEffect
primariesPer-ability primary stat + strength. ability is the ability defName.
empowersPer-edge stat + strength for a source→target link.
sourcesReplace a target ability's entire synergy-source list with from.
statAny SynStat name (Power, Radius, Duration, Strength, Range, Cooldown, Targets, Charges, Efficiency, Haste, Yield, ProjectileCount, SummonCount), case-insensitive, or none for no scaling. Omit to leave the auto/baked stat.
strengthScaling multiplier (1 = auto). Omit to leave the auto/baked strength.

Every field is optional - supply only what you want to change. Later-loading defs win a key over earlier ones (last-wins). Players can also round-trip a shared JSON in-game: mod settings → Specializations → Import balance edits (from JSON) reads any file the Share button exported into the Psycasts2 save-data folder back into their live overlay.
